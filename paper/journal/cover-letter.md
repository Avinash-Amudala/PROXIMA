Please consider “PROXIMA: Auditing Proxy Decisions and Within-Segment Disagreement in Online Experiments” for publication in IEEE Access.

The manuscript describes an auditable diagnostic for proxy metrics in online controlled experiments. It distinguishes overall decision agreement from within-segment proxy disagreement, states the meaning and limitations of a weighted summary, and evaluates the resulting trade-offs using independent synthetic training and test experiment corpora. Component ablations and simple decision policies reveal both useful behavior and a calibration-shift failure. An observed-data illustration using Criteo visit and conversion outcomes is explicitly limited to a pooled treatment comparison.

An earlier version is available as arXiv:2604.14352. This submission substantially revises its definitions, implementation, references and numerical evaluation. The earlier headline results are replaced, and the revised manuscript does not present generated recommendation outcomes as independent real-world validation. Reproduction code, aggregate results and the detailed protocol are available in the accompanying repository.

The author has reported that the work is not under consideration by another journal and has declared no conflicts of interest. The manuscript discloses the use of OpenAI Codex in research software and manuscript revision.
